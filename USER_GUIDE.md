# Car Explorer Dashboard – User Guide

## Overview

This application allows users to explore a dataset of cars with filtering, search, sorting, and export functionality.

---

## Searching

- Use the search bar to find cars by make or model

---

## Filtering

- Select:
  - Make
  - Region
  - Year

---

## Sorting

- Sort cars by:
  - MPG
  - Horsepower
  - Year

---

## Pagination

- Use Previous / Next buttons to navigate through pages

---

## Saving Preferences

- Your filters and search settings are automatically saved
- When reopening the app, your previous settings are restored

---

## Export Data

- Data can be exported as CSV via the backend API:
  - `/api/cars/export`

---

## Notes

- Data may not load temporarily if Firebase quota is exceeded
